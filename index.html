No signal? Keep recording.

BoothTap works offline, without an account, ads or analytics. Choose USD, GBP, CAD or EUR for each event. You decide when to share an export.

BoothTap records amounts; it doesn't process payments, calculate taxes or track inventory. “Sales less booth fee” excludes your other costs.
