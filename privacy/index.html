BoothTap / Privacy

Your records.
Your control.

Effective October 17, 2026. This policy covers the BoothTap iOS app and this support website.

Information in the app

BoothTap stores event names, currencies, dates, booth fees, recorded amounts, correction history and preferences locally on your device. We do not collect these records or transmit them to our servers. The app has no account, analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Exports and backups

You choose whether to export and share CSV files. Destinations you select, such as email or cloud storage, handle the files under their own policies. Your device's operating system backup settings may include app data. BoothTap does not operate a cloud backup or sync service.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you send. We use it to respond and troubleshoot, and do not use it for advertising. Do not send private sales information unless it is needed and you choose to share it. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us.

Retention and deletion

App records remain on your device until you remove the app or its data. Deleting the app can delete the records. Export reports you need first. Contact us to request deletion of information you provided through support, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

This website

This static website uses no analytics scripts or advertising cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection information to operate and secure hosting; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

BoothTap is a business recordkeeping tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the policy changes. For privacy or support questions, contact Stelyra-Astro@proton.me.
