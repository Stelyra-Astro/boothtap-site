BoothTap / Support

A little help
for market day.

Contact Stelyra-Astro@proton.me. Include your app version, iPhone model and a description of the issue. Share sales records only if you want to.

How do I record a sale?

Start Market Day, choose a currency and optionally add your booth fee. Tap a quick price to save one entry. Custom accepts another amount or a whole order. BoothTap does not collect payment.

Can I change the price buttons?

Choose Edit prices in Market or Quick prices in Settings. Keep 1–8 prices, drag to reorder, or remove a price. Custom remains available. Each currency keeps its own price list.

What does “entry” mean?

One saved amount. You decide whether that represents one item or a whole order. It is not a reliable count of customers, orders or physical items.

I tapped the wrong price.

Use Undo to void the latest active entry. In All entries, use Void or Restore to correct another record. Voided entries stay visible and are excluded from totals. These actions do not process refunds.

Why isn't the total called profit?

Sales less booth fee subtracts only your booth fee from recorded sales. It excludes materials, travel, payment fees and taxes. Enter amounts using a consistent convention.

How do I export my records?

Open an event in Events to see its Summary, then choose Export CSV. Select Files or another destination in the system share sheet. Entry rows include recorded/voided status; filter to recorded when summing amounts. Summary rows use metric and value columns.

How do I add, edit or settle an event?

Tap + in Market or Events to add an event, even while another event is open. In Market, Edit beside the event name changes its name and booth fee. Settle ends the selected event after confirmation and keeps its records in Events.

Can I continue an old event?

Yes. Open a past event in Events and choose Reopen event. You can keep several events open. Choose Record sales in an event’s Summary, or tap the event name in Market to switch where you are recording. An event's currency is fixed, and midnight does not automatically end it.

Are records synced or backed up?

There is no BoothTap cloud sync. Your device backup settings may include app data. Deleting the app can delete its records. CSV exports are reports; CSV import and restore are not supported.

My records won't open.

Free device storage and retry. Don't uninstall the app before contacting support, because that can remove local data.
